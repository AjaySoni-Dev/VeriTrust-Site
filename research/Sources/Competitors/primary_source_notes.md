# Competitor primary-source notes

Research cutoff: 2026-08-16. Only public product/API/documentation claims are recorded. Proprietary model topology, unreported latency, retention, accuracy and pricing are marked unknown rather than inferred.

The sources show a consistent market pattern: enterprise email products combine multiple weak signals, relationship context, threat intelligence, pre/post-delivery action and analyst workflows; URL-intelligence APIs separate lookup from policy; media-detection vendors use asynchronous uploads, ensembles and explainability; provenance systems deliberately avoid claiming truth; mature awareness platforms connect observed risk to targeted learning. Those observations directly drive VeriTrust’s evidence graph, explicit missingness, human review, async receipts, provenance lane and risk-linked learning roadmap.
